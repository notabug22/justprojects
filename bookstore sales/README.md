OBJECTIVE:
Analise book sales, checkout trends, pricing patterns, and publisher performance to understand factors influencing bestseller performance and overall bookstore revenue.
DATASET:
Bookstore transaction dataset containing:
o	Book titles
o	Authors and series
o	Genres
o	Publishers
o	Pricing and discounts
o	Checkout dates
o	Ratings
o	Marketing expenditure
o	The dataset allows exploration of both sales performance and content popularity patterns.
TOOLS USED :
o	Tableau Public
o	Calculated Fields
o	Filters and Parameters
o	Dashboard Actions
o	KPI Indicators
o	Interactive Visualizations
DASHBOARD COMPONENTS:
1. SALES OVERVIEW:
Provides a high-level summary of bookstore performance.
Includes:
•	Total Sales
•	Total Orders
•	Number of Books in catalogue.
•	Month-over-Month Sales Growth
•	Monthly Sales Trend
•	Checkout activity over time
•	Interactive filters allow exploration by:
•	Genre
•	Publisher
•	Sale Date
2. PRICE AND MARKETING ANALYSIS:
Examines how pricing and marketing investments influence sales.
Key visualizations include:
•	Price of Books table
•	Discount vs Sales scatter plot
•	Publisher ROI comparison
•	This analysis helps identify whether:
•	discounts drive higher sales
•	marketing spend translates to revenue
3.POPULARITY AND AUTHOR INSIGHTS:
Focuses on content-level performance.
Includes analysis of:
•	Top 10 most checked-out titles
•	Top performing authors
•	Series popularity and sales
•	Relationship between writing time and book ratings
These insights help understand:
•	what type of books performs best
•	which authors generate consistent demand
•	how series-based books influence sales
KEY INSIGHTS:
•	A large share of bestselling titles belong to book series.
•	Sci-Fi / Fantasy titles show high checkout frequency.
•	Discounts do not show strong correlation with higher sales.
•	Highly priced books often have lower sales volume.
•	Writing time of authors does not show a strong relationship with book ratings.
•	Marketing spend varies widely across publishers but does not always translate to higher ROI.
BUSINESS VALUE:
This dashboard can help publishers and bookstore managers
•	Identify high-demand genres
•	Evaluate effectiveness of marketing investments
•	Optimize pricing strategies
•	Understand author and series popularity
•	Monitor monthly sales performance
